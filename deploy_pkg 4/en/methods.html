Methods

Field protocols, data processing, SEEA EA framework

1. Study area

Southwest Madagascar, Atsimo Andrefana region, Ampanihy, Toliara II and Morombe districts. Toliara reef complex, ~80 km (Sarodrano–Andavadoaka). 36 villages, 86 ecological monitoring stations, six ecological zones (Soariake, Teariake, Paysage Mahafaly, Baie de Ranobe, Sambele, Velondriake), four governance regimes (Nosy Ve Androka MPA, Ranobe Bay MPA, Velondriake LMMA, Soariake LMMA).

2. Reef extent (EC1)

Benthic classification from an atmospherically corrected Sentinel-2A image (Sen2Cor/SNAP), processed with a depth-invariant index (Lyzenga) to limit the water-column effect, segmented into homogeneous objects (OBIA, eCognition), classified by k-nearest-neighbours (k=5, Euclidean distance, inverse-distance weighting). Validated against georeferenced CPCe photoquadrats, aggregated into five standardised classes (Coral/Algae, Seagrass, Sand, Rubble, Rock).

The opening baseline combines two sources depending on the zone: Nomenisoa et al. (2021) within the Grand Récif de Toliara, Allen Coral Atlas (2021, 5 m Planet resolution) outside the GRT, with a transition matrix computed separately per zone before area-weighted aggregation.

Documented precision targets: overall accuracy ≥ 75%, coral producer's accuracy ≥ 70%, user's accuracy ≥ 75%, confusion-matrix validation on 50 to 100 independent field points, formalised quality control (class totals within ±1%, filtering of polygons < 0.5 ha).

3. Condition — fish (EC3, Pillar 1)

UVC belt transect, 50 m × 5 m (2.5 m on each side), i.e. 1,250 m³ per transect, two transects per station, 5-minute acclimation period, two successive passes (mobile/pelagic species then benthic/cryptic species).

W = a × L^b

where W is weight (g), L is total length (cm), grouped into 5 cm size classes, and a, b are species-specific allometric coefficients (FishBase; McClanahan et al. 1996).

Indicator Reference level Source
Fish biomass 600 kg/ha (sustainability threshold) McClanahan et al. 2016
Species richness 117 spp./station (WIO median) Samoilys et al. 2019
COTS density (inverted) 15 ind/ha (outbreak threshold) Dulvy et al. 2021
Coral recruitment 1–3 recruits/m² Hughes et al. 2010

4. Condition — invertebrates (EC3, Pillar 2)

Two distinct protocols. Urchins: circular 10 m² quadrats (178 cm radius cord), placed every 10 m along three 50 m transects, i.e. 5 quadrats/transect and 15/station — hence the 50 m²/transect area retained in the final calculation (a documented gap with the 250 m² value stated verbally by the field team, probably confused with the fish protocol, unresolved). Other macro-invertebrates (holothurians, giant clams, octopuses, lobsters): 50 m × 1 m zigzag transects, no substrate manipulation, i.e. 150 m² cumulated over a station's three transects.

Indicator Reference level Confidence
Urchin density 1.5–2 ind/m² (WIO, provisional) LOW
Giant clam abundance (Tridacna maxima) 131 ind/ha (Apte et al. 2010, Lakshadweep) LOW — underestimates observed densities
COTS (Acanthaster planci) Qualitative presence/absence (n insufficient for density) Moran 1986; Pratchett et al. 2014; Westcott et al. 2020

5. Socio-economic (Pillar 2)

Exhaustive village-level census of fishers and gear (13,672 fishers, 8,508 canoes, 21 communes, Faustinato Behivoke). Landings sampled twice a month over 12 months, accounting for tidal cycles, taxonomic identification at family level. Household survey in two waves with different recall instruments (VF1: single-trip recall; VF2: 7-day diary) — a methodological confound confirmed statistically, VF2 retained as the reference for CPUE/GVA. CPUE estimated following Laurec and Le Guen (1981).

6. Monetary valuation

Benefit-transfer approach for reef services: unit value in USD/ha/yr by benthic class, multiplied by the area of each class within each spatial unit (MPA, LMMA).

For the broader ocean economy (OESA), informal fisheries valuation is presumed to follow a resource-rent / opportunity-cost approach (Barnes-Mauthe et al. 2013), distinct from the market price used for formal SNA GVA — a distinction that conditions the interpretation of the consistency ratio between informal GVA and the MECA.

7. Normalisation of condition indices

General formula, identical to the one used by the comparable Kenya Coastal Ecosystem Accounts:

CI_i = min(x_i / x_ref,i , 1.0)

For inverted indicators (pressure, degradation):

CI_i = max(1 − x_i / x_threshold , 0)

Discipline point applied in this pipeline (and absent from the public documentation of the comparable account): every new CI formula is accompanied by a range assertion checking that the result stays within [0, 1] before any aggregation — the absence of this check in a comparable account let through uncapped formulas (DBH, height) capable of producing a CI > 1.

The integrated M1.3 composite weights by pillar (fish 0.40; invertebrates 0.20; benthic 0.40) rather than a simple average, with an explicit partial-account flag (compte_partiel) when an entire pillar is missing — currently the case for Pillar 3 (benthic habitat).

8. SEEA EA framework alignment

  • Ecosystem types: photic coral reefs (IUCN GET M1.3)
  • Accounting area: Toliara reef complex, Atsimo Andrefana, Madagascar
  • Account types: Extent (EC1, pilot), Condition (EC3, partial), Services (EC4, blocked), Asset and monetary (EC2/MECA, blocked)
  • Accounting periods: 2021–2026 (extent); continuous 2025-2026 campaign, treated as a single period rather than two independent campaigns (condition); 2025 proposed as the pivot year (MECA, not confirmed)

9. Known limitations

  1. Full benthic classification not independently validated — unreconciled area gap (~4,587 km² vs. 111,544 ha canonical), validation metrics partly built on training data.
  2. Site-level benthic habitat unavailable — Pillar 3 of the condition account pending, M1.3 composite currently partial.
  3. Household survey instrument confound (VF1/VF2) — limits the temporal comparability of informal CPUE/GVA.
  4. Repeated cross-sectional design, not a panel — each village observed 1–2 times, limiting CPUE trend analysis.
  5. Urchin sampling area not confirmed by the field team — 50 m²/transect retained on biological plausibility grounds, at odds with the verbal value of 250 m².
  6. Non-discriminating IC_clam — reference value (Apte et al. 2010) too low relative to locally observed densities.
  7. COTS sampling artefact — a single individual observed on a single transect can convert into a density that wrongly triggers the outbreak threshold.
  8. Unassigned ecological zones for four stations (Nosy Satra, Befasy, Ambohibola, Tanilahy) — fieldwork closed, not resolvable through new collection.
  9. Level-4 accounts (services, asset, OESA) blocked — dependent on a consensus extent account not yet reached.
