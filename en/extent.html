Reef extent account — EC1 (M1.3)

Toliara reef complex, Southwest Madagascar

Account status: Pilot (canonical layer validated) — the full benthic classification intended to feed the condition account (Pillar 3) remains Blocked, see Cross-ecosystem synthesis.

Canonical baseline

Total extent (2021, revised 2026)

111,544 ha

Reconciliation gap

8.88 ha ≈ 0.008% of the baseline

Benthic classes

5 Coral/Algae, Sand, Rubble, Seagrass, Rock

The Reef_extent_SW_Mada_OASISM.csv / Reef_extent.shp layer is the pipeline's canonical reference, validated to within 0.006% after deduplication. It is distinct from the full benthic classification output (Resultat.shp, see Status of the full benthic classification below), which is not retained as-is.

Composition by benthic class

SEEA EA point of attention: the Coral_Alga class merges live coral and macroalgae (Harris et al. 2009). The SEEA EA framework normally requires these to be separated for the extent account and for differentiated monetary valuation — this is not yet the case in this pilot.

Ecological zones and governance regimes

Six ecological zones structure the accounting area: Soariake, Teariake, Paysage Mahafaly, Baie de Ranobe, Sambele, Velondriake. Four governance regimes overlay these zones: two marine protected areas (Nosy Ve Androka, Ranobe Bay) and two LMMAs (Velondriake, Soariake).

The disaggregation of extent into hectares by ecological zone and by governance regime (protected vs. open access) is not yet available as a finalised pipeline output — see extent-protected-area in the data tables. This absence of disaggregation should not be confused with an absence of governance: the MPA/LMMA table exists but its area cells are not filled in at this stage (Blocked status).

Status of the full benthic classification

The full classification output (Resultat.shp, 9 classifier layers) remains at Blocked status:

  • Total declared area after topological correction: ~4,587 km², roughly 4x above the canonical baseline (111,544 ha ≈ 1,115 km²) — a gap not reconciled in the response received.
  • Validation sample (1,822 points): 1,437 points (79%) are in fact training points reused for validation. The reported metrics (OA=95.77%, Kappa=0.8899) are therefore not independent of training, despite being presented as such.
  • The origin of two classifier layers (DTCX, RTCX) is disputed by the classification's declared author — a disagreement not resolved against the source file's own content.
  • The only substantive and usable point in the response received: the baseline strategy combining Nomenisoa et al. (2021) within the Grand Récif de Toliara and the Allen Coral Atlas (2021) outside the GRT, with a Coral/Algae → Sand transition matrix of 18% (GRT) and 14% (outside GRT), aggregated to 16% (± 3 points).

Consequence for the condition account: Pillar 3 (benthic habitat, 40% weight in the integrated composite) remains pending until these points are resolved — see Reef condition.
