Integrated SEEA EA accounts

Four-level architecture, M1.3 — photic coral reefs

The pipeline follows a four-level architecture, each level strictly dependent on the previous one, combining SEEA-EA (UN 2021) for extent/condition/services, SEEA-CF (UN 2014) for physical and monetary flows, and the GOAP (2022) methodology, with the ultimate aim of SNA/NAMAD compatibility via INSTAT.

Level Account Skill Status
1 Extent (EC1) extent-measurement Pilot — canonical baseline 111,544 ha; full classification blocked
2 Condition (EC3) condition-ecosysteme-integree-m13, condition-biotic-fish-invert Partial — Pillars 1+2 (fish, invertebrates) available; Pillar 3 (habitat) blocked
3 Services (EC4) services-provisionning, services-nursery Blocked — VF1/VF2 confound, missing commune→district crosswalk
4 Asset and monetary (EC2 / MECA) asset-account-m13, monetary-ecosystem-account-m13 Blocked — depends on a consensus EC1 (not reached) and a resolved EC4 (not reached)
4 (satellite) Ocean Economy Satellite Account (OESA) comptabilite-oceanique Blocked — all GVA line items pending
Planned integration path: Extent account (ha) → Condition account → Services account (physical flows) → Monetary valuation (USD/ha/yr × ha) → OESA (GVA, blue jobs, ocean share of regional GDP). Each link in this chain depends on the resolution of the previous level — not yet the case as of July 2026.

OESA-1 table (pilot, July 2026)

The "memorandum" line item (value of reef ecosystem services, MECA) is never added to the total ocean GVA and must never be duplicated with the "informal fisheries" line. Its status remains Blocked: EC3 invertebrates moved to Pilot in July 2026, but EC1 extent is not resolved by the response received — the MECA remains blocked until extent has a verified, not merely declared, consensus layer.

Provenance of the "informal fisheries" line

The provisioning valuation method is presumed by default to follow a resource-rent / opportunity-cost approach (Barnes-Mauthe et al. 2013), consistent with what is mobilised for the MECA (Case A). Under this presumption, the ratio of informal GVA to EC4 MECA would be a legitimate consistency indicator — but the consistency range currently documented in the skill ([0.3; 3]) is calibrated on an external case (Randriambelo thesis, factor ≈ 3.4) identified elsewhere as containing an unresolved GDP/production-value inconsistency. This calibration is therefore circular and should not be treated as a validated threshold.

Methodological gaps resolvable without further fieldwork

  • Written formalisation of the services-provisionning method (resource rent, Case A) — largely resolved in practice, still to be documented.
  • Verification of δ=1 fisheries/aquaculture (exclusion of inland aquaculture) in the DRPEB breakdown.
  • Non-overlap of DRPEB / customs for formal fisheries — risk of double counting if unverified.
  • Circular [0.3; 3] consistency range — needs an independent derivation (Barnes-Mauthe cost structure) or a replacement.
  • Single MECA reference year (2025 proposed) — administrative decision to be validated.
  • Coastal tourism / local maritime transport sharing convention — to be fixed before Phase 1.

Empirical gaps — require field collection or external data

  • Informal fisheries GVA: unresolved VF1/VF2 confound, missing commune→district crosswalk.
  • Tourism/transport δ coefficient: Phase 1 (proxy) not launched, Phase 2 (primary survey) not budgeted.
  • Finalised MECA (USD figure): depends on a consensus EC1, still blocked.
  • Reconciliation of total classification area (~4,587 km² vs. 111,544 ha canonical): ~4x gap unexplained by the response received.
  • INSTAT regional accounts (Atsimo Andrefana GDP): not identified as available to date.
