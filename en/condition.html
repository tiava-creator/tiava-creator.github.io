Reef condition account — EC3 (M1.3)

Three pillars: fish, invertebrates, benthic habitat

The integrated M1.3 condition account normally weights three pillars (fish 0.40; invertebrates 0.20; benthic 0.40). Since Pillar 3 (benthic habitat) is pending (see Extent), the composite CI is partial regardless. It is currently also blocked: as of 2026-08-07, the COTS indicator is excluded from the calculation (presence/absence treatment retained, no extrapolated density — see Pillar 2 below) and the species-richness value to use is under internal dispute (see box below). Neither 0.76 nor 0.455 (earlier versions) should be cited as the current composite CI.

Pillar 1 — Fish (UVC, 26 sites / 86 stations)

Fish biomass

367.3 kg/ha Official calculation reference: 1150 kg/ha (conservation target, McClanahan et al. 2016) — CI = 0.32 For reference (contextual, not used in the calculation): WIO sustainability threshold 600 kg/ha — CI = 0.61

Species richness

58.4 spp./station WIO median 117 (Samoilys et al. 2019) — CI = 0.50
Methodological correction to always keep in mind: the current biomass value (367.3 kg/ha) corrects a factor-2 error in an initial estimate of 744.5 kg/ha (incorrect transect-area assumption). Never reuse 744.5 kg/ha as a reference in a figure or draft.
Two biomass reference levels coexist deliberately. 1150 kg/ha (WIO conservation target) is the official reference used in the CI and integrated composite calculation — CI_biomass = 0.32. 600 kg/ha (sustainability threshold) is shown for context only, to situate the reef relative to the sustainability floor (0.61); it is never used as a denominator in a published calculation. This clarification corrects an internal discrepancy found on 2026-08-07 between this page (which previously showed only the 600 threshold) and the pipeline skill (which uses 1150).
Unresolved internal discrepancy on species richness. This page shows 58.4 spp./station (per-station average). An earlier version of the pipeline documentation used 55 spp. with no traceable source, and a third output file (per site, not published here) gives per-site richness of 30 to 94 spp. depending on the zone. These three figures are not necessarily the same quantity (per-station average vs. cumulative per-site richness). Until Laza or the raw field data settle the definition and value to use, no M1.3 composite CI should be published as a final value.

The canonical hierarchy retained is the precomputed biomass by site/station (source: Laza), adopted as authoritative — the pipeline does not restart from the raw CSVs using the "one village = one site" convention. Site-level biomass detail is not yet published as a finalised pipeline output; only the zone-level aggregate (unweighted mean) is retained as publishable at this stage.

Pillar 2 — Invertebrates (323 post-QA rows, 6 zones)

IC_urchin by ecological zone

Substantive change not to be missed: Sambele's IC_urchin has collapsed from 0.8757 (old, superseded) to 0.0648 after ecological reassignment of four stations (Fierenamasay, Tsandamba) from Sambele to Baie de Ranobe. Sambele moves from a profile close to Velondriake to the most degraded zone in the account after Teariake. Any document using the old value of 0.88 must be corrected.

Sensitivity — Ambohibola/Tanilahy (Paysage Mahafaly)

A 5.6% gap on IC_urchin (Paysage Mahafaly) depending on whether the Ambohibola/Tanilahy stations — whose ecological assignment is not directly confirmed — are included or not. Impact judged moderate; working assumption retained as-is.

IC_clam — a limitation of the index, not a sign of good condition

IC_clam is capped at 1.0 in all six zones without exception. The reference value used (131 ind./ha, Apte et al. 2010, Lakshadweep) is too low relative to the densities observed here (250 to 967 ind./ha): the index currently discriminates no zone at all and should not be read as excellent condition everywhere — this is a documented limitation of the reference value, not an ecological result.

COTS (Acanthaster planci) — qualitative presence/absence only

Approach retained (decision by Tiava, confirmed by Laza citing Moran 1986; Pratchett et al. 2014; Westcott et al. 2020): qualitative presence/absence, no extrapolated density in ind./ha. Presence confirmed only in the Paysage Mahafaly zone (3 sites: Anakao, Beheloky, Retsela; 4 observations). The absence of COTS in the other five zones should be read as a protocol gap (no dedicated manta-tow or timed search), not as confirmed ecological absence.

This presence/absence decision has a direct consequence for the M1.3 composite CI: a qualitative indicator cannot enter a 0–1 average alongside quantitative indicators. As of 2026-08-07, COTS is therefore excluded from the composite calculation (which moves from 4 to 3 candidate indicators: biomass, richness, urchin) rather than forced into the formula via a density judged statistically indefensible.

Pillar 3 — Benthic habitat

Status: Blocked / PENDING. No site × year data on coral cover, algae, or rubble is available at the level of the condition sites. The full Sentinel-2 classification (Resultat.shp) feeds the extent account, not the site-level condition account, and carries the same area defect documented on the Extent page. The M1.3 composite cannot therefore be computed with its full 40/20/40 weighting until this pillar is delivered.

Methodological artefact to flag

Three sites (Anakao, Beheloke, Retsela) show a converted COTS density of 40.0 ind./ha derived from a single observation on a single 0.025 ha transect (1/0.025 = 40). With only 2 transects per station, a single Acanthaster spotted by chance is enough to flip a site from "control baseline" to "outbreak alert" under a naive reading. This confirms the methodological choice retained here (presence/absence, not point density) for this rare, spatially aggregated species.

Output file not to be used

A file named EC3_condition_integree_m13_par_zone.csv / _par_site.csv (generated 2026-08-07) circulates in the working folder with IC_urchin values that differ from the canonical table above for Paysage Mahafaly (0.54 instead of 0.63) and Teariake (0.08 instead of 0.03). Checked directly against IC_Invertebres_OASIS-M_v2_corrige.xlsx (sheet Zones_IC): the table shown on this page is the confirmed reference. The source of the discrepancy in that file has not been investigated — do not reuse it until it has been.
