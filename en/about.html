About

Partners and acknowledgements

Institut Halieutique et des Sciences Marines (IH.SM), University of Toliara

IH.SM leads the field data collection and ecological monitoring underpinning the OASIS-M pilot account. Field teams coordinated underwater visual censuses (UVC) on the reefs, benthic invertebrate surveys, two waves of household surveys, and a fishers/gear census across 36 villages of the Toliara reef complex between November 2024 and 2026.

Key collaborators:

  • Yves Amoros Mitondrasoa — GIS, remote sensing, benthic classification
  • Laza — field lead, fish and invertebrate UVC
  • Maka — field data collection, invertebrate monitoring
  • Faustinato Behivoke — fisheries surveys, household data, MPA/LMMA governance tables
  • Nomenisoa — reef baseline (Grand Récif de Toliara, 2021)

Co-authors of the project's formal documents: Todinanahary Gildas, Behivoke Faustinato, Mitondrasoa Yves Amoros, Mandimbilaza Tsiresimiary.

Global Ocean Accounts Partnership (GOAP)

The Global Ocean Accounts Partnership is a multi-stakeholder initiative supporting countries in measuring and managing progress toward sustainable ocean development through standardised ecosystem accounting. GOAP is a recognised UN Ocean Decade Action. OASIS-M (Ocean Accounts in Support of Sustainable Ocean Planning for Madagascar) is part of GOAP's East Africa programme, alongside the comparable Kenya Coastal Ecosystem Accounts pilot (Kilifi County), with which a detailed methodological comparison is available on the Cross-ecosystem synthesis page.

UNSW Sydney

The GOAP Secretariat, hosted at UNSW Sydney, provides technical and methodological support to the project: natural capital accounting framework, SEEA EA account structure, regional networking.
