Ecosystem services account — EC4

Nursery and fisheries provisioning (Vezo artisanal fisheries)

Overall status: Blocked for a final figure. The methodological architecture (anti-double-counting rule, data-quality decision tree, value-change decomposition) is nonetheless more advanced than in comparable accounts in the region — see Cross-ecosystem synthesis.

Architecture of the two service accounts

The OASIS-M pipeline distinguishes two reef-linked service accounts, with an explicit rule preventing the same physical flow from being counted twice:

  • Provisioning (services-provisionning) — wild fish supply, artisanal catch and effort, valued at resource-rent price (Case A, presumed by default, Barnes-Mauthe et al. 2013).
  • Nursery (services-nursery) — regulating service: juvenile habitat, Local Recruitment Ratio (LRR), biomass enhancement attributable to the reef's nursery function (CICES 6.1.1).

An explicit anti-double-counting check table documents where the provisioning boundary ends and where the nursery boundary begins, to avoid counting the same physical flow twice in the aggregated monetary account.

Provisioning — blocking status

The physical and monetary provisioning account depends on two elements currently blocked:

  1. VF1/VF2 instrument confound. Household survey wave 1 (377 households, single-trip recall, Toliara II only) and wave 2 (525 households, 7-day diary, three districts) are not comparable — a confound confirmed statistically in the Efoetse commune, the only one covered by both instruments (Mann-Whitney, p = 1.7×10⁻⁶). Decision taken: the reference (headline) CPUE/GVA is anchored exclusively on VF2; VF1 is retained as descriptive only.
  2. Missing commune → district crosswalk. Needed to link UVC sites to WDPA zones (see extent-protected-area) and to disaggregate fishing effort by gear — not available at this stage.

Correction factor for undeclared catch

The f_correction factor retained (range [2, 5]) is cross-checked against locally published CPUE data: Behivoke et al. (2026) — 2.5 kg·h⁻¹; Laroche & Ramananarivo (1995) — 0.6–2.0 kg·h⁻¹. This local anchoring is a more robust methodological point than the equivalent used in comparable accounts in the region (a fixed factor not validated against a local CPUE reference).

Nursery — not yet calculated

The nursery account has no computed value at this stage. Its construction depends on having at least two simultaneous time points for condition and extent (to allow the ΔV decomposition below) — a condition not currently met.

If adjacent ecosystems (mangroves, seagrass) are present within the accounting area, the attribution of the nursery service between ecosystems must be delimited before finalisation (see adjacent-ecosystems-scope) — not addressed in this pilot, as no mangrove/seagrass data has yet been integrated into the M1.3 boundary.

Value-change decomposition (tool available, not yet applied)

The pipeline has a formal decomposition of the year-on-year change in service value:

ΔV = ΔV_CI + ΔV_extent + ΔV_price

This tool would make it possible to distinguish a real ecological effect (change in CI), an area effect (change in extent), and a market effect (change in price) in any observed change in catch or service value. It has not yet been applied for lack of two simultaneous time points on CI and extent.

This decomposition does not exist in the public methodological documentation of the Kenya account (Gacutan et al. 2026), which reports a 172% increase in catch between 2020 and 2024 without being able to determine whether this reflects stock recovery, better reporting, or a change in effort. If a dialogue channel exists with the Gacutan/COMRED/CCVA team, this formula is directly transferable to their own question.
